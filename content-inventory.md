## Brand and entity
- Legal / brand: Partners for Growth (PFG); Partners for Growth Managers, LLC (privacy controller).
- Positioning: specialty growth debt and asset-backed financing for high-growth tech, fintech, healthcare, and tech-enabled companies.
- Tagline themes: “Partners for Growth”; “We go where the entrepreneurs are.”
- Tenure claim: team behind PFG active ~35 years in tech/innovation financing; firm founded 2004 by Andrew Kahn and Don Campbell (formerly Hambrecht & Quist Guaranty Finance).
- Registered investment adviser (disclosed on social media / disclosures pages).

## Primary offers and product facts
- Core product language: **growth debt** (broader than typical venture debt).
- Structures: term loans, lines of credit, royalty / revenue-based financing, asset-backed warehouse facilities.
- Typical ticket: loans **$1-50MM**.
- Borrower profile: revenue **$5-150MM**, or asset portfolios **$5MM+**.
- Transaction requirements (stated): security in assets (senior or subordinate); equity participation (warrants/conversion); financial covenants.
- Differentiators vs venture debt (their framing): company-centric underwriting (credit, assets, path to profitability); open to VC, PE, family office, or bootstrapped; timing tied to strategy not only equity rounds; used to accelerate profitability or finance assets, not only extend runway.
- Sector focus: Software (SaaS, cloud, cyber, data); Fintech (payments, lending, proptech, mobility); Healthcare; Infrastructure; Hardware; Consumer / e-commerce.
- Asset-backed specialty: warehouse funding across currencies, asset classes, jurisdictions (esp. fintech receivables).
